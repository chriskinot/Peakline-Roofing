Our Story
1988

A legacy of craftsmanship

My name is James, and I am a second-generation roofer with over 15 years of hands-on experience. Following in my father's footsteps, I proudly continue the family business he established in 1988 — providing reliable, professional roofing services with a strong focus on customer satisfaction.

We specialise in leak detection and guttering services because they offer an affordable solution for many homeowners, helping to prevent costly damage before it becomes a bigger problem. We always work closely with our customers to find the best solution that fits their budget without compromising on quality.

Based on Station Road, Huthwaite, we serve Mansfield and Nottinghamshire with pride. Our friendly team provides free, no-obligation quotes with honest advice every step of the way.

What We Do

Our Services

We specialise in roofing repairs, leak detection and guttering — affordable solutions that protect your home before small problems become expensive ones.

🔧

Roof Repairs & Re-Roofs

Leaks, broken tiles, damaged flashing — we diagnose and fix issues fast. Full re-roofs using quality materials for lasting protection.

💧

Leak Detection & Repair

We specialise in finding and fixing roof leaks. Accurate diagnosis saves you money and prevents long-term structural damage.

🏠

Guttering & Replacements

Leaking gutter repairs and full gutter replacements. Keeping water away from your home with quality UPVC and seamless guttering.

🔍

Roof Maintenance & Inspections

General roofing maintenance and thorough inspections with honest advice. Included free as part of every quote we provide.

🏗️

Fascias & Soffits

UPVC close-down detailing, fascias, soffits and bargeboards — protecting the roof edge and giving your home a clean, finished look.

⚡

Emergency Call-Outs

Storm damage or sudden leaks? We're available quickly across Mansfield and Nottinghamshire to get your roof secure.
